Happy Birthday! ♡

First of all, HAPPY BIRTHDAY!! I hope you smile a lot today, eat something you really like, and feel extra loved — because you deserve that and so much more.

I'm really glad you're in my life. Sometimes I might not say it properly, or I might act like it's no big deal, but having you as my best friend means a lot to me. I'm thankful for all the talks, the laughs, the random nonsense, and even the little moments that might seem normal but end up staying in my memory.

I hope this new year of your life brings you peace when things feel heavy, courage when you're unsure, and happiness that doesn't need a reason. I hope you keep growing into the person you want to be, and that you never feel like you have to become someone else to be worthy of love.

And on the days when life doesn't go the way you planned, I hope you remember that one difficult day doesn't define your whole story. Take your time, be kind to yourself, and don't forget how much good you bring into the lives of the people around you.

Here's to more memories, more laughter, more silly conversations, and more moments that we'll look back on and smile about. I hope this birthday is the start of a year that treats you gently and gives you plenty of reasons to feel proud of yourself.

Happy birthday once again, Bhaji. I'm cheering for you, always. ♡

— your best friend ✦
